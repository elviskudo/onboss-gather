# Space 2 — Harvest Farm

Space 1 retains the existing Hallstatt 3D village. Space 2 is a separate interactive 3D scene with a grassy farm, dirt paths, vegetable beds, cottage, red barn, greenhouse, workshop, well, fences, fruit trees and animals. Architecture and animals use original smooth 3D meshes. Vegetation, terrain and small props retain the user-supplied pixel-art sheet, sliced into 37 alpha assets with nearest filtering; building slices remain as source references only. Buildings, trees and the well have independent shared server/client collision proxies. Character avatars remain articulated 3D, deliberately without facial features; age is communicated through proportions, hair and black/white beards. Female presets include hijab, niqab and a child hijab.

The camera is perspective with an elevated farming-game view. Vegetation/prop sprites use bottom-centered pivots and camera-facing planes in world space; ground textures tile at two world units, with a four-unit low-contrast grass layer. Art is sourced from `assets/source/farm-atlas.jpeg` (user upload 2), with its SHA256 and exact slices in `space-2-assets.json`. Upstream license metadata was not supplied; no claim of original authorship is made for these supplied sprites. Transparent normalization is deterministic flood-fill of neutral light JPEG/checkerboard pixels connected to crop borders. White interior details remain intact.

Run `python3 scripts/prepare-farm-assets.py` to regenerate. Inspect `space-2-contact-sheet.png` and live Space 2 at actual scale. No AI-generated substitutes are used for the supplied environment art. Characters from the sheet are not used: the requested faceless configurable 3D avatars are shared by both spaces.


## Proportion and living-herd revision

The reference screenshot showed full-height crops competing with the human avatar and inconsistent prop scaling. Space 2 now uses compact articulated humans (78% body scale, a larger head-to-body ratio, age/hijab/niqab silhouettes retained), a closer default camera, smaller ground rings and labels anchored above the actual farm silhouette. Environment sprites preserve the source image aspect ratio. Houses and trees frame the avatar; crops are roughly knee-height, fences about waist-high, and mailbox/crate/well sizes are reduced. A soft opaque grass base plus a 28% atlas grass layer reduces visual noise. The compact header and animal HUD leave the scene readable on desktop and mobile.

The living herd is code-authored smooth 3D geometry: 3 cows, 4 sheep, 3 cats, 6 chickens and 5 ducks. Species share warm cream/brown/orange materials, smooth surfaces, small eyes and articulated heads/legs/tails. Cows have spots/horns and a broad muzzle; sheep have wool clusters; cats have pointed ears and raised tails; chickens have combs; ducks have flattened bills. The dog is absent from both the scene and public crop pipeline. Original user atlas is retained unchanged for provenance.

Animals patrol deterministic walkable routes with walking and grazing phases. A server-authorized meal freezes the current patrol position for eight seconds without teleporting, lowers the head, shows a food bowl, and resumes the route afterward. Shared server-clock offsets and persisted meal records synchronize this across users and reloads without a server tick loop. Click an animal or choose it from the herd list, approach, then use the feed button or F. The server checks space, building, distance and cooldown.

`src/FarmAnimals.ts` is the editable model/rig source; `shared/animals.ts` owns species, names, routes and timing; no external model or generated-image license is required for these code-authored animal meshes. Verify real 3D rig motion and bounds with `node --test tests/animal-models.mjs`; inspect full-resolution screenshots from `tests/farm-npcs.spec.ts` before publication.


## Natural 3D architecture revision

All six structures are authored in `src/FarmBuildings.ts`: plaster cottage, red timber barn, glazed greenhouse, timber workshop, chicken coop and open stone well. No building uses a sprite, image plane or external model. Original metre-scale geometry includes full-depth walls, bevelled gable roofs, roof courses/ridge caps, foundation plinths, corner timbers, window frames, plank doors, chimney caps, barn loft vent/braces, greenhouse glazing/frames/raised beds, and a hollow well with water, posts, rope and pitched roof. Materials react to the existing sun/sky lighting. The colour family follows the supplied farm reference without copying its pixels onto building billboards.

Buildings start at world Y=0.65 and retain the authoritative `shared/farm.ts` footprint, so NPC paths and player collision remain consistent. Cottage/workshop walls are 2.95 m, barn walls 3.5 m, coop walls 2.05 m; human entrances are at least 1.25 × 2.35 m, with a smaller chicken-coop door. Opaque pieces merge per material per building for a bounded draw-call cost; greenhouse glass remains transparent. Four building roots retain room metadata; mesh raycasts resolve the nearest ancestor entrance, preserving click-to-enter and meeting/whiteboard interiors.

Run `node --test tests/farm-buildings.mjs` to verify all six models have actual depth, finite geometry/normals, grounded bounds, appropriate scale, collision footprints and clickable human door fronts. `tests/farm-buildings.spec.ts` renders high-quality architecture from two angles and visits all four meeting/whiteboard interiors. NPC tests independently verify the herd and feeding are preserved.
